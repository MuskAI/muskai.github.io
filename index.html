Haoran Chen

Master's Student in Computer Science, Zhejiang Gongshang University

Stay hungry, Stay foolish.

I am a Master’s student in Computer Science at Zhejiang Gongshang University. My research focuses on how multimodal systems select, combine, and preserve visual information.

research interests

Multimodal Language Models. Visual layer selection, multi-layer feature fusion, and connector design for more effective vision-language models.

AI-Generated Content Detection. Robust detection and analysis of AI-generated content, with an emphasis on practical and generalizable methods.

goals & plans

  1. Bring “ai” (a Chinese pinyin meaning “love”) and AI to the world.
  2. Expand AI services to millions of people through commercial applications.
  3. Share findings and insights from my research journey to advance practical applications.

open to discussion
